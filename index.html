FAQ

Questions, answered

What does "Auto Apply" actually mean?

Auto Apply is a one-click action. Pick a role you like, click Auto Apply, and we fill out the application and attach a cover letter tailored to that job using your resume. You choose every job — nothing is ever sent without your approval.

How do you find my matches?

We scan millions of verified listings daily. Our AI compares your role, experience, skills and preferences against each job's actual requirements and shows you the best-fit roles first.

Do you guarantee interviews or offers?

No tool can guarantee an employer's response. What we guarantee is that every application you send is real, tailored and matched to your profile. Many users see activity within the first couple of weeks, though results vary by market and role.

How do I cancel?

Cancelling takes under a minute from your account — no phone calls, no retention screens. You keep full access until the end of your billing period and won't be charged again.

Will employers know I used AI?

No — employers see a normal, professionally written application. Your cover letter is built around your own experience and your resume stays yours.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, and used only to power matching and applications. Delete anything — or everything — anytime from settings.
